About VS Photographs

I’m the photographer behind VS Photographs, based in Modesto, California.

I photograph portraits, events, landscapes, and commercial work with a simple goal: make photographs that feel genuine, intentional, and worth keeping.

Whether it’s a family session, a special event, a place you want remembered, or images for your business, I want the experience to be straightforward and the finished photographs to speak for themselves.

If you have an idea for a session or project, I’d love to hear about it.
