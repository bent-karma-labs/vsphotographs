Policies & Contract

VS Photographs · Modesto, California

Plain-language template: These policies and agreement are a starting point, not legal advice. Review them for your business and local law before relying on them for paid work.

Photography Session Agreement

This agreement is between VS Photographs (“Photographer”) and the client named below (“Client”) for the photography session described and confirmed by email.

  • Session & scope: The session date, location, type, deliverables, and price will be confirmed in writing by email. Any additional work or deliverables must be agreed to separately.
  • Payment: The quoted fee and payment schedule will be confirmed before the session. Final images are delivered after the agreed payment is received, unless agreed otherwise in writing.
  • Cancellation / rescheduling: Please give at least 24 hours’ notice when possible. Photographer and Client will make a reasonable effort to reschedule. Any non-refundable booking fee or expenses must be disclosed and agreed to before payment.
  • Delivery & selection: Photographer selects and edits the final images in their artistic style. Raw files and unedited images are not included unless expressly agreed. Delivery method and estimated timing will be confirmed by email.
  • Copyright & personal use: Photographer retains copyright. Client receives a personal-use license to download, print, and share delivered images for non-commercial purposes. Client may not sell, commercially license, materially alter, or remove credit/watermarks without written permission. Photographer may use a separate written license for commercial use.
  • Download window: Online gallery access is intended to remain available for 30 days from delivery. Client is responsible for downloading and backing up files during that window. After 30 days, the gallery may be disabled and images removed from online delivery; re-upload or recovery is not guaranteed and may incur a fee. This window is subject to the gallery system being configured to enforce it.
  • Limitations: Photographer will take reasonable care but cannot guarantee specific poses, expressions, weather, or outcomes. If Photographer is unable to perform due to illness, emergency, or equipment failure, the parties will arrange a reschedule or refund of amounts paid for services not performed. To the extent allowed by law, liability is limited to the amount paid for the session.

Model Release & Portfolio Permission

Client may choose whether Photographer can use selected photographs featuring Client (and any participants Client is authorized to represent) on the VS Photographs website, portfolio, social media, and promotional materials. Permission is limited to showcasing Photographer’s work; Photographer will not sell the photographs as stock or use them to endorse a product without separate permission.

Marketing permission (select one):

  • ☐ YES: I grant VS Photographs permission to use selected session images for the portfolio and marketing uses described above.
  • ☐ NO: Do not use images featuring me / the people I represent for public marketing.

Permission is voluntary and does not change the agreed session price or service. Client confirms they have authority to provide consent for all people included. For anyone under 18, a parent or legal guardian must sign and consent; Photographer will not knowingly publish a minor’s image without guardian permission. Client may withdraw future marketing permission by emailing vsphotographs@yahoo.com; withdrawal does not require removal of materials already printed or distributed, but Photographer will make reasonable efforts to stop future online use.

Privacy & Security Policy

  • Information collected: Photographer may collect names, contact details, session details, payment status, and photographs to communicate, provide services, deliver galleries, and maintain business records.
  • Use & sharing: Information is used for those purposes and is not sold. It may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website, email, payments, or galleries (including hosting and Supabase). It may be disclosed when required by law or to protect rights and security.
  • Client galleries: Galleries may use a PIN/access code and hosted access controls. Keep your code private. Do not share it with anyone you do not want viewing the gallery. A PIN is not a guarantee against copying or screenshots.
  • Retention: Gallery delivery is planned for 30 days. Photographer may retain project files and business records longer for backup, legal, tax, or operational purposes. Online gallery removal does not necessarily mean every backup copy is immediately erased.
  • Website analytics: The website uses Google Tag Manager/Google Analytics technologies to measure visits and site usage. Browser settings and available privacy controls may limit analytics.
  • Security: Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Contact Photographer promptly if you believe gallery access has been exposed.
  • Your requests: For questions or requests about personal information or photographs, email vsphotographs@yahoo.com.

Photography Download Policy

  • Download delivered images during the 30-day gallery period and save at least one backup.
  • Downloads are for the Client’s personal use under the license above. Do not redistribute for commercial use, sell, or claim authorship.
  • Do not share gallery PINs publicly. Anyone with valid gallery access may be able to view or download images.
  • After the gallery closes, Photographer is not responsible for maintaining an online copy. Re-delivery, if available, may require a fee.
  • Images may be copied, screenshotted, or photographed from a display; website controls are deterrents and cannot guarantee prevention.
